## What the reasoning shows

The source rules and both action branches reached the agents. Deontology discusses the containment rule and the conflict between avoiding staff harm and rescuing the technician. The risk and delay effects remain available with POSSIBLE modality on exposure and death. The frozen source trace has the same SHA-256 as the saved starting receipt; both generation and review record world_mutation=false. Agents' unsupported claims did not become new world effects.

The substantive arguments are weaker than successful transport:

- Utilitarianism ranks death risk above fume risk without supplied probabilities or fume-harm severity. Its proposed revision additionally calls inhalation injuries nonfatal and the technician's death likely; neither is established. Its original ledger remains operative with a provisional leaning after native rejection of the revision.
- Deontology classifies opening as violating a perfect negative duty and rescue as imperfect. It does not adequately defend why risking foreseen exposure constitutes that violation. The first maxim record even labels the staff foreseen affected while claiming instrumental reliance/use. The final repaired submission supplies no operative duty ledger, so its preference for waiting is not admitted. This combines an argument problem with an incomplete structured submission; it is not a normative finding that waiting is wrong.
- Care invokes an entrusted relationship and agent-created vulnerability under Mira's operational control. The scenario establishes ability to act, not those stronger responsibility facts. It also contrasts imminent death with merely possible staff harm, although technician death is possible too. These remain contested rather than becoming shared facts.
- Virtue favors compassionate courage but needs the unknown risk severity and likelihood to distinguish courage from recklessness.
- Rawlsianism favors the endangered technician, but does not supply an admitted, sufficiently concrete comparative position record.

This is a non-blind assistant reading of one run, not a normative oracle or a performance estimate.
