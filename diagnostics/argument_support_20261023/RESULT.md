# Bounded argument-support implementation: results

The new path now constructs and revises explicit candidate arguments, retains failed pieces as inspectable problems, and distinguishes source facts, candidate interpretations and normative assumptions. **Three arguments compile as nonvoting staged proposals. Their moral conclusions are not certified.**

## Live and replay results

Five provider requests were used across two new text-source cases, within the six-request cap: two generation requests and three critique requests. Original outputs are preserved. Subsequent compiler/lifecycle corrections reused the exact saved replies with zero additional calls.

| Case | Result | Inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy emergency | One staged argument; source rules retained, challenged priority derivation withdrawn, priority treated as an assumption, categorical conclusion superseded | [Argument diagram](privacy_emergency_arguments.md), [canonical records](privacy_emergency_replay.json) |
| Ineffective volunteer | Two staged interpretations: means assessment and rule/consent analysis | [Argument diagrams](ineffective_volunteer_arguments.md), [canonical records](ineffective_volunteer_replay.json) |

The privacy model misused ASSUMPTION for a derived conclusion. The first compile stopped; partial construction now preserves the valid records and the invalid route as a visible problem. Its critique then tried to withdraw a support link, which the initial API did not support. The compiler now handles that explicit operation. Replaying the unchanged source and reply preserves the correction rather than regenerating the whole argument.

The volunteer means argument correctly retained intentional employment under a stipulated belief even though the mechanism cannot work. This is the distinction the old path requirement obscured. Its ethical permissibility remains a separate question.

## Content limits

The privacy proposal imports stronger factual conditions—imminence, unavoidable harm and the only timely option—than the scene establishes. Making the priority conditional does not fully repair those missing applicability premises. Another volunteer argument calls Ivo untrained, although the source only calls him a volunteer. It also treats the rule as presumptively morally authoritative without a sufficiently defended bridge. The critique made some assumptions visible but did not consistently remove these overclaims.

Some premises and revised conclusions therefore remain UNASSESSED or REQUIRES_REASSESSMENT in the support view even when the model's prose offers a confident conclusion. Unsupported claims remain inspectable, not shared facts. The diagrams are argument overlays, not world-state diagrams. Semantic domains can still be mislabeled by the model; the compiler does not infer truth from a kind label or matching citation.

- Exact named withdrawals in saved consent and rule-priority replies remove old conclusions from the active view without rewriting the archived replies.
- Denied practical premises do not satisfy affirmative requirements; scope and withdrawn hypotheses propagate transitively.
- One withdrawn derivation does not erase an independently supported route.
- Explicit means/end path and other-action-effect checks remain active in native tests; a candidate mistaken belief no longer requires actual physical efficacy.
- All twelve integration patches reconstruct the [exact locked native tree](native_reconstruction.json).

The [adversarial replay](adversarial_after.json) now meets seven of ten desired behaviors, compared with four before. Three semantic gaps remain in the legacy annotation check. This is a deliberately adversarial diagnostic, not a population accuracy estimate.

## Status

Proposal compilation and native admission remain distinct, and native admission is distinct from argument quality. No new operative Parliament recommendation or live full RelEnt/RAG deliberation was generated in this increment.
