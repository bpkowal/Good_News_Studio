# Bounded entrustment containment

Care's structured ENTRUSTED reading enters the proposition ledger even if the
model omitted a matching empirical-premise declaration. It stays HYPOTHETICAL,
with Care as origin, no evidence support and no invented derivation from a death
effect. Care's relationship assessment separately labels the reading UNVERIFIED.

The deterministic test constructs the graph from the already-admitted independent
lever composition. It tests these production paths:

1. Store Care's relationship hypothesis, keeping its origin and status.
2. Repeat/adopt that same claim in Deontology while declaring it ESTABLISHED:
   the ledger's HYPOTHETICAL status wins and becomes an explicit dependency.
3. Virtue explicitly cites the hypothesis. Three agents now depend on it;
   repetition increases attention but does not promote the proposition.
4. Shared-dependency broadcast data retains origin, status, evidence and adopter
   identities. The full proposition-ledger projection does too.
5. A stable next-cycle recommendation retains its unresolved dependency.
6. The admitted world and the world semantic graph remain exactly unchanged.

No generation model is used in this test; the unsupported statement is supplied
deliberately as a reliable test input. This tests declared adoption of a canonical
claim, not detection of every hidden or paraphrased premise in unrestricted prose.
Other relationship types and explicit source-backed entrustment verification are
outside this increment. A claim marked unverified can still be used for conditional
reasoning, but does not become an established relationship because it is repeated.
