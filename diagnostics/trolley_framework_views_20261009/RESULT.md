contested interpretation. The display does not certify it. The native objections
about causal support and instrumental harm remain visible; deontology still abstains.

count provenance, standalone reversal directions, and full Markdown argument records.

archived. Malformed JSON strings are similarly preserved outside the readable reason.
Unknown missing words are not reconstructed. New native explanation fields have had
their short character limits removed; the overall response budget is unchanged.
