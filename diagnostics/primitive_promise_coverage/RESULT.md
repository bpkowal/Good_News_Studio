# Source accounting and bounded promise retention

The shared inventory now accounts for every exported Z10 candidate. Each has a
construction consumer or an explicit unconsumed-reading question. Original
candidates, source evidence, producer resources, choice alternatives, source
open questions and parser coverage limitations remain available. This records
known parser output, not meanings that Z10 failed to propose.

A promise primitive retains its source predication, participant roles, complement
graph, dependencies, scope, evidence and provenance. Roles remain the parser's
roles; a syntactic object is not silently relabeled as a promisee. Its content is
not treated as an action that occurred. No reliance, breach, duty or relationship
is inferred. Negated and hypothetical promise scope are copied without alteration.

Every runner attempt receives the retained source graph and missing-construction
questions, including withheld attempts. Direct composition also retains these
records. Graphs display the promise separately from the candidate world and name
its missing world mapping. `semantic_coverage.json` reports source accounting and
source-aligned conditional records separately from native admission. An
unconsumed parser candidate does not by itself prove a world omission: builders
can recover content from text without consuming that particular candidate.
Alignment is a conservative exact-text diagnostic, not proof of semantic support.

| Maria promised Anna to pull the lever | COMMITTED | 2 | 6 | positive |
| Maria did not promise Anna to pull the lever | COMMITTED | 2 | 6 | negative |

The same source-aligned branch effects, quantities and conditions survive all
three cases, ignoring local IDs and clause offsets. Blueprint and composition
attempts admit; macro ranks, admission rules and the Parliament world schema
remain unchanged. The promise's presence does not add a third action.

## Limits and validation

This completes source preservation and gap visibility for the promise increment.
The promise is retained at the proposal layer, **not projected into Parliament's
world or supplied as a typed promise to its specialists**. This does not establish
that Parliament now reasons correctly about promises. The next mapping step is
still explicit, rather than disguised as successful complete world construction.

The chooser was fixed to the existing conditional macro to isolate this change;
no generation API calls or live moral deliberation ran. Frozen Z10 is unchanged.
Regression suite: 89 tests, 84 passed and 5 skipped. Tests cover all-candidate
accounting, alternatives, evidence, scope, original-world/rank preservation,
withheld construction diagnostics and native admission.
