Justice in holdings depends on three historical conditions—just acquisition, just transfer, and rectification of past violations—rather than any patterned end-state distribution.

> “A distribution is just if it arises from another just distribution by legitimate means… The entitlement theory consists of three principles: justice in acquisition, justice in transfer, and rectification of injustice in holdings.”
