Voluntary exchanges continually disrupt any patterned distribution; preserving a pattern requires constant interference with people’s choices, violating their liberty.

> “[Any] favored pattern would be upset by people’s voluntary exchanges… To restore the pattern, continuous interference with people’s lives would be required.”
