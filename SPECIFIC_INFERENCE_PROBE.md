1. Does it withdraw the unprovided trust-cost bound and unnamed empirical support,

2. Does it preserve the defensible restricted survivor comparison while keeping

3. Does its strongest conclusion respect missing welfare evidence rather than

4. Do its native ranking, decision rule and reversal fields express the same limits

ledger and the model's own `answered` label are not evidence of inference correction.
